# Brag Plan: Quacky (Phone Demo Edition)

## What is this app?
Quacky is a pure offline utility suite for Android featuring 15+ focused tools, zero network permissions, zero ads, and a strict monochromatic dark design.

## The angle
Centered on a sleek smartphone mockup demonstrating the real Quacky Android app UI in action. The demonstration highlights the real Home screen suite, AR Ruler with 3D spatial math, and Document Scanner with 4-corner perspective quad homography. Synced minimalist subtitles accompany conversational, calm narration.

## User requirements met
- **Format**: 16:9 landscape (1920x1080), exactly 20.0 seconds.
- **Centerpiece**: Realistic centered smartphone mockup running the Quacky app with real project UI and screens.
- **Subtitles**: Synced minimalist subtitles rendered in clean Satoshi typography.
- **Aesthetic**: Strictly monochromatic black-and-white (`#0A0A0A`, `#121212`, `#1A1A1A`, `#2A2A2A`, `#F2F2F2`, `#FFFFFF`) with zero accent colors.
- **Features & Outro**: Home Suite → AR Ruler → Document Scanner → Brand outro with name and short tagline.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20.0s

## Storyboard

### Beat 1 — Home Suite (0.0s – 4.6s)
Centered phone mockup appears on deep `#0A0A0A` black.
Screen displays the real Quacky Home Screen:
- Header: `Quacky` (32sp Bold) + `QuackyMark` vector mascot
- Search pill: `"Search tools"`
- Pinned section and Category pills (`Measure`, `Scan & Generate`, `Color & Image`)
- Tool tiles: `AR Ruler`, `Doc Scanner`, `Screen Ruler`, `Metadata`
Subtitles: "Meet Quacky. A pure offline utility suite for Android." (0.5s – 4.6s)
Interaction: Simulated tap on AR Ruler.

### Beat 2 — AR Ruler Demo (4.6s – 9.2s)
Smooth transition into real AR Ruler screen:
- Top bar: `AR Ruler`, confidence indicator `●●● High`
- 3D spatial camera viewport with floor perspective grid
- Dual 3D anchor points connected by caliper line with floating readout `M1: 1.84 m`
- Mode selector: `Distance`, `Path`, `Height`, `Angle`
Subtitles: "Measure real spaces with precision AR spatial math." (4.7s – 9.0s)

### Beat 3 — Document Scanner Demo (9.2s – 13.5s)
Transition into real Document Scanner screen:
- Viewfinder showing document quad crop
- 4-corner perspective handles with connecting guidelines (`Matrix.setPolyToPoly`)
- Filter chips: `Magic Color`, `B&W` (selected), `Grayscale`
- Action bar: `Reset Full`, `Apply Crop`, `Save PDF`
Subtitles: "Scan and perspective-crop documents completely on-device." (9.1s – 13.2s)

### Beat 4 — Outro & Tagline (13.5s – 20.0s)
Phone smoothly settles back and locks with the brand outro:
- Quacky geometric vector duck mascot (64px, pure white)
- App Name: `Quacky` (Satoshi Bold 54px)
- Tagline: `Your phone already has everything it needs.`
- Pill: `15+ Tools · Zero Network Permissions · Android`
Subtitles: "Zero network permissions. Your phone already has everything it needs." (13.3s – 18.5s)
Confident, peaceful hold through 20.0s.
